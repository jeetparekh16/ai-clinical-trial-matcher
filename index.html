Which trials could this patient join?

Describe the patient the way you would in a referral note. Each trial’s eligibility criteria are turned into rules and checked one by one, so you can see exactly why a trial fits or not.

Or read the patient from an EHR

Signs in with SMART on FHIR, the standard EHRs like Epic and Cerner use for apps, and reads conditions, labs and medications. The default server is the public SMART sandbox with synthetic patients.
